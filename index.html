The stats

Know why you won. Or why you didn't.

Every point records how it ended, so the summary shows what to work on.

How it works

Logging a point takes one tap

Tap or press your half of the screen for our point, theirs for their point. Tap again straight away to say how it ended.

×1

Point

Score, serve and games move on by themselves.

×2

Winner or error

Credited to you. Our half is a winner, their half an error.

×3

For your partner

The same, credited to your partner.

Stats are optionalOnly want the score? Turn shot stats off and every tap is simply a point. Or track your own shots without your partner's.
