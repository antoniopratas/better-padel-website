Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Better Padel keeps the score of your padel match on your watch. It's made by Antonio Pratas. This page explains what the apps and this website do with your data. The short version: the apps don't collect anything about you, and nothing you record leaves your watch or your own Apple or Garmin account.

The Apple Watch app

  • Match data stays on your watch. Scores, settings and an unfinished match are stored on the watch only.
  • Apple Health. With your permission, the app reads your heart rate during a match and saves each match to Apple Health as a workout, with the result and score. That data is held by Apple Health under your control, and you can change or remove the app's access at any time in the Health settings on your iPhone. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.
  • No accounts, analytics, tracking or ads. The app has no servers and makes no network requests.

The Garmin app

  • Match data is stored on your watch. Each match is saved as an activity on your watch and in your Garmin Connect account, through Garmin's own services, under Garmin's privacy policy.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads, and sends nothing to us.

This website

  • If you register your interest, we store your email address, which watch you play with, the watch model (if you give it) and the date. Sign-ups are handled by Formspree.
  • We use them only to send you one email when Better Padel launches. We don't share or sell them. Ask us to delete yours at any time.

Children

Better Padel isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
